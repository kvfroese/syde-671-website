In the great city of Waterloo, a lone victim, walking in the evening come autumn time, encounters a road that seemingly obeys non-physical properties in some freakish horror of nature. After an indeterminate period, she wakes to find herself hostage to a cat with a lot of weapons. But turns out the cat is pretty nice. However, in an utter tragedy, she is terrible at taking selfies, they just never come out right!
